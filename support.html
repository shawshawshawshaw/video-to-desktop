FAQ

Which links can I use?
Direct links to video files you own or are authorized to use (for example .mp4, .mov or .m3u8). Web pages of video sites are not supported.

Where are downloaded videos saved?
Free: inside the app. Pro: in any folder you choose in Settings → Storage.

How do I restore my purchase on another Mac?
Open Settings → Membership and click “Restore Purchases” while signed in with the same Apple ID.

The wallpaper pauses when a full-screen window covers it.
That is intentional to save power; audio keeps playing and video resumes when the desktop is visible.

可以用哪些链接？
你拥有或已获授权的视频文件直链（如 .mp4、.mov、.m3u8），不支持视频网站的网页地址。

下载的视频保存在哪里？
免费版保存在应用内部；会员可在 设置 → 存储 中选择任意文件夹。

换一台 Mac 如何恢复购买？
使用同一 Apple ID 登录，打开 设置 → 会员，点击「恢复购买」。
