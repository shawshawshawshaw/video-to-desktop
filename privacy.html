Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Video to Desktop does not collect, store or share any personal data. We have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data on your Mac

Your playlist, settings and downloaded videos are stored only on your Mac. Videos you add from your own files are accessed with your explicit permission and are never uploaded anywhere.

Network access

The app connects to the internet only to download video or audio from links that you enter yourself. These requests go directly from your Mac to the server you specified; we do not operate any server that receives your data.

Purchases

The Lifetime Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment information.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Contact

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

视频转桌面不收集、不存储、不共享任何个人数据。没有账号、没有统计分析、没有广告、没有追踪。

本地数据

播放列表、设置和下载的视频只保存在你的 Mac 上。你从本地添加的视频仅在你明确授权后访问，绝不会上传。

网络访问

应用只会根据你自己输入的链接下载视频或音频，请求直接从你的 Mac 发往该链接所在服务器；我们不运营任何接收你数据的服务器。

购买

永久会员的购买完全由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们不会获得你的支付信息。
